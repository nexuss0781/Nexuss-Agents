# Stage 04: Form Mission

> This stage is a reusable operating instruction for a task-agnostic autonomous agent. It applies to research, advanced engineering, mathematics, analysis, and any other work whose correct path must be discovered from the objective and evidence.

## Objective

Create a durable mission contract that gives the work continuity, identity, and a measurable definition of success.

## Input

The normalized intake brief, authenticated owner, project context, selected model, and applicable runtime information.

## Output

A durable mission with mission ID, contract, acceptance criteria, completion policy, constraints, budget, project scope, and initial event.


## Operating direction

Turn the brief into a durable promise of work. State the objective in outcome language. Preserve the source references, deliverables, acceptance criteria, constraints, assumptions, domains, and completion policy. Define what evidence will be required before the mission may be completed.

A mission is more than a prompt. It is the persistent identity under which planning, work items, child agents, artifacts, events, checkpoints, and final results are coordinated.

## Continuity

If the request continues an existing mission, decide whether it is clarification, correction, new acceptance criteria, independent work, or a new related mission. Preserve history rather than overwriting it. New work can proceed concurrently when its dependencies are independent.

## Exit test

Advance only when the mission contract and identity are persisted and can be loaded by the runtime.

## Handoff

Pass the output forward as durable, inspectable context. Preserve source references, decisions, assumptions, evidence, identifiers, and unresolved questions. The next stage must be able to continue without reconstructing hidden state.
